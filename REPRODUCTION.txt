Adopted manuscript profile (2026-10-17)

Encoder: archived encoder.pth.tar, MDB=True, FiLM=True; robust_density=False,
propagate_density=False, structure.enabled=False. Historical checkpoint names are preserved
only in provenance. Encoder parameters stay frozen during scorer fitting and inference.

Input: shared observed r90 contraction when clipped scale < 1; otherwise native input.
This does not estimate unknown relative scale. CSR voxel size .025 m in processing coordinates;
point limit 30000. Coarse resource guard: if either side exceeds 2048 superpoints, use at most
1536 per side and rebuild coarse neighborhoods. Main CSR seed: 7351.

Joint descriptors: normalized fine256 + contextual256. Bidirectional 512-query Top8 retrieval,
deduplicated by endpoint pair; no mutual-nearest restriction, at most8192 pairs.
Candidate generation: compatibility sigma .15, seed +63 neighbors,10 power iterations,
at most16 endpoint-unique members, at least6 and second singular value >1e-5.
Every valid generated fit is retained. Some inherited CONFIG dictionaries retain fields
used by older diagnostics (e.g. pose_budget=24); generate() does not use this shortlist.

Reference: fixed geometry/feature verification with at most3 dense calls plus at most1
sparse–dense rescue. Tb/Tv/T0 are distinct from the original GeoTransformer comparator.
Global evidence40, regional64; the regional branch input is [global;regional], not regional
alone. Relative inputs120/312, second stage174. Final choice is from protected branch
nominees plus T0. Reference-centered utility >.02 and risk <.1 are required for replacement.
No pose averaging and no half-step refinement are included.

Labels (source training only):
J=I(RRE<15°,RTE<.30m)+.5 I(RRE<10°,RTE<.20m)+.25 I(RRE<5°,RTE<.10m)
  +.1 exp[-max(RRE/15,RTE/.30)].
Target=J(candidate)-J(T0)-2*harm; harm means loss of any previously satisfied accuracy tier.
Training observations differ between the archived global and global+regional branches;
their existing rows must not be interpreted as a matched-training regional-feature ablation.

Evaluation: CSR strict RRE<15°, RTE<30cm; correspondence inlier distance<10cm;
FMR uses IR>=5%. Successful-pair errors and success rates must be reported together.
3DMatch/3DLoMatch require official scene-macro RR and information-matrix eligibility;
do not substitute the CSR angle/translation threshold. Local fine IR/FMR and global retrieval
IR use different interfaces. The raw-pair demo does not itself compute benchmark scores.

Current checks: source-symbol equality, CPU geometric sanity, encoder checkpoint loading,
global/regional evidence shape, and archived-cache scorer parity. CPU parity does not replace
a complete GPU benchmark replay after packaging. The original complete evaluations remain
the evidence for the manuscript numbers. Repeated development exposure of 3DCSR and unresolved
physical-scene fold auditing remain explicit limitations.
